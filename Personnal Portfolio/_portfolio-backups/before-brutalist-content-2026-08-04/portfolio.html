Professional bio

Pierce John A. Vargas is a creative professional developing work under the identity of Life and Tech. His portfolio is centered on modern visual presentation, thoughtful design, and polished digital communication.

His interests include design, photography, visual storytelling, and digital presence. He values projects that are well-composed, purposeful, and refined in detail.
